## Measuring visits and advertising (Meta Pixel and Conversions API)

Inwora Technologies ("we") uses the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. ("Meta"), to measure visits to inwora.com and how well our advertising on Facebook and Instagram works.

**Only with your consent.** These tools run only if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Decline", or make no choice, nothing is sent to Meta. You can change your choice at any time from "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of every page.

**What is sent to Meta when you accept:**

- that you viewed a page, opened a product or case study, or clicked a WhatsApp button, with the page address (without any text after a "?") and the name of the product, case study or button;
- your IP address and browser type (user agent);
- Meta's cookie identifiers (`_fbp`, and `_fbc` if you arrived from a Meta ad).

**What is not sent:** your name, email address, phone number, or the content of any WhatsApp message. A click on a WhatsApp button is recorded only as "opened WhatsApp", not as an enquiry.

**How it is sent:** from your browser by the Meta Pixel, and from our server through the Conversions API, using the same event identifier so Meta counts each event once.

**Cookies:** after you accept, Meta sets first-party cookies on inwora.com (`_fbp`, `_fbc`). When you decline after accepting, we stop sending data and remove these cookies from inwora.com.

**Meta's use of the data:** Meta processes this data under its own terms and data policy: [link to https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy]. [Confirm whether your Meta Business account uses Meta's Data Processing Terms / restricted data use settings.]

**Retention:** we do not store this data on our servers beyond short-lived technical records (a hashed form of your IP address and of each event identifier, used to limit abuse and duplicates, normally deleted within a few hours). [Confirm Meta's retention period to state here.]

**Your rights:** you can withdraw consent at any time via "Cookie preferences". For any other request about your personal data, contact [name / role] at info@inwora.com.

**Contact:** Inwora Technologies, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India. [Street address when confirmed.] Email: info@inwora.com.
